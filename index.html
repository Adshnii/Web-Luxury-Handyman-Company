A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we get started.

What should I include in my request?

Tell us your city or ZIP code, what needs to be done and your preferred timing. Photos and measurements help us understand the project. You can attach photos in your email or WhatsApp message.

How is the price confirmed?

Pricing depends on the scope, materials and conditions of the job. Contact us to discuss your project and confirm pricing before scheduling.

When can I contact you?

Our business hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and Saturday, 8:00 AM–2:00 PM, Eastern Time. We’re closed on Sunday. Appointment availability is confirmed directly.
