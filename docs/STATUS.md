## Approved presentation

- English website, continuous graphite background and white text, approved company logo, framed illustrative interior photograph.
- Headline: “Your home. Our care.” Navigation: Services, About us, Let’s talk.
- Four hero cards: Minor repairs, Hanging & mounting, Maintenance, Other projects.
- Services, About us, FAQ and contact sections; service cities appear below contact hours.
- About us describes more than five years of hands-on experience, work in EU countries and extensive US experience, quality, ongoing communication and visit planning.
- Working hours: Monday–Friday 08:00–18:00, Saturday closed, Sunday 08:00–16:00, Eastern Time. Client contact availability: 24/7.
- Form order: name, location, email, project details. Locations: Naples, Bonita Springs, Marco Island and Other location with a required custom input.
- “Send my email” opens the visitor’s email application with prepared content. The visitor reviews and sends it there. No automatic email delivery or server-side storage.
